# TrendWave | Modern Luxury Contemporary E-Commerce Website

Welcome to **TrendWave**, a modern, minimalist, ultra-premium e-commerce web platform designed for contemporary street luxury, technical outerwear, engineered footwear, and lifestyle tech accessories.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Color Palette

Designed around an **Ultra-Light Grey & Off-White Contemporary Luxury Aesthetic** inspired by Scandinavian and Tokyo design studios (such as Acne Studios, Fear of God Essentials, and high-end technical fashion):

- **Background Canvas:** Off-White / Ultra-Light Grey (`#f8f9fa`, `#f1f3f5`, `#ffffff`)
- **Text & Contrast:** Deep Obsidian Charcoal (`#121316`) and Architectural Slate (`#5c626d`)
- **Accents:** Matte Black (`#0f1115`), Subtle Warm Highlights (`#d97706`), Emerald Success (`#16a34a`)
- **Borders & Dividers:** Whisper-light architectural grey borders (`#e9ecef`, `#dee2e6`)
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean geometric legibility paired with `Playfair Display` for editorial accents.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Functionality

### 1. Header & Navigation
- **Dynamic Announcement Bar:** Promotes worldwide shipping with coupon code `TREND20` (dismissible).
- **Sticky Glassmorphism Header:** Frosted navigation bar with subtle blur on scroll.
- **Brand Identity:** Geometric wave insignia with bold contemporary typography.
- **Live Badges:** Real-time counters for both Shopping Bag and Wishlist.
- **Mobile Responsive Drawer & Menu:** Fully adaptable to smartphones, tablets, and wide monitors.

### 2. Editorial Hero Section
- High-fashion editorial photography showcasing current seasonal drops (*Autumn / Winter '26*).
- Floating glassmorphism micro-cards highlighting fabric certifications (`Custom 480 GSM Cotton`, `Express Worldwide Dispatch`).
- Social proof cluster displaying 45,000+ happy global creators and 4.9/5 verified ratings.

### 3. Trust & Brand Value Proposition Strip
- **Express Logistics:** Complimentary tracked 48h dispatch on orders > $150.
- **Conscious Craft:** GOTS certified organic cotton & recycled technical fabrics.
- **30-Day Risk-Free:** Simple self-serve returns and exchanges.
- **Encrypted Security:** 256-bit bank-grade encryption with Apple Pay and Google Pay.

### 4. Bento Grid Collections Showcase
- Curated visual bento layout for core categories:
  - *Heavyweight Streetwear & Boxy Silhouettes*
  - *All-Weather Technical Shells*
  - *Titanium Precision Horology & Tech Gear*
  - *Supercritical Foam Runners & Footwear*

### 5. Interactive Storefront & Product Catalog
- **Category Filter Pills:** Instant filtering by *All Drops*, *Streetwear*, *Outerwear*, *Footwear*, *Accessories*, and *Tech Gear*.
- **Live Search Bar:** Real-time keyword search across garment names, descriptions, and tags.
- **Sort Dropdown:** Sort by *Featured Drops*, *Price: Low to High*, *Price: High to Low*, and *Customer Rating*.
- **Interactive Product Cards:**
  - High-res photography with hover zoom effects.
  - Category and status badges (*Best Seller*, *New Drop*, *Sale -15%*, *Winter Edition*).
  - Quick-add to bag button with rotating animation.
  - Interactive wishlist heart toggle with local storage persistence.
  - Hover "Quick View" modal trigger.

### 6. Quick View Product Modal
- Detailed product image gallery.
- Comprehensive specifications, GSM ratings, and material details.
- Interactive color swatch selector (switches live color name).
- Interactive size picker (`XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `US 8-12`).
- Fit guide link.
- Quantity stepper (+ / -).
- Add to Bag with dynamic price preview and toast notification.

### 7. Slide-Over Shopping Bag (Cart Drawer)
- Slide-in side drawer with backdrop blur.
- **Dynamic Free Shipping Meter:** Live progress bar calculating remaining dollars until complimentary express shipping is unlocked.
- Per-item quantity controls (+ / -) and instant item removal.
- **Voucher / Promo Code Engine:**
  - Enter `TREND20` for 20% off entire order.
  - Enter `WAVE10` for 10% off.
- Live subtotal, estimated shipping, discount calculations, and grand total.
- Instant "Proceed to Secure Checkout" button.

### 8. Interactive Checkout Flow & Simulation
- Clean modal with customer contact, shipping address, and simulated payment method selection (Card, Apple Pay).
- Order summary with computed discounts.
- Order authorization simulation that generates a unique tracking code (e.g., `TW-583921`) with order confirmation screen.

### 9. Editorial Lookbook with Hotspots
- Editorial fashion lookbook featuring interactive "+" hotspot pins.
- Hovering over pins displays product mini-cards.
- Clicking any pin opens the Quick View modal directly for that featured piece.

### 10. Verified Customer Reviews & VIP Club
- Verified buyer testimonials with star ratings.
- VIP Insider newsletter form with instant 20% discount toast notification.

## 🚀 How to Open and View the Website

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser:
- Double click `index.html` in your file explorer, or
- Right click `index.html` -> **Open with** -> Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Firefox.

Alternatively, if you prefer running a local development server, run:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then navigate to `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
